About us

We bake sourdough, pastries and cakes every morning. The coffee is local, the produce is seasonal, and the tables are for anyone who needs a quiet hour.

Our Claremont room seats 30 for private events. The Fremantle courtyard is dog-friendly. The Northbridge store stays open late on Fridays.
